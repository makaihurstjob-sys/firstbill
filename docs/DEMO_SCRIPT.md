# FirstBill: 30-second screen recording

Setup: open the live demo on a laptop, full screen, light mode. Do one practice run first. If the page asks
to let the artifact use Claude, allow it so the dialog doesn't pop up while you record.

Easiest: click **Play full demo, hands-free** and record the phone. It runs on its own in about 40 seconds.

What to say over it (about 30 seconds):

1. (Phone shows "Text JOIN to 34778") "Tariq just graduated. He texts JOIN. That's the whole download."
2. (Link arrives, signup opens) "FirstBill sends one link: his name, payday, his StudentAid.gov loan file, and a read-only bank link through Plaid. Sixty seconds, then it's all texts."
3. ("6 months later", the notification drops in) "Six months later he hasn't thought about his loans once. FirstBill texts him first."
4. (Timeline card with the red overdraft row) "His grace period ends in 12 days. His first payment, plus his phone bill and car insurance, would overdraft him by twenty-three dollars before payday."
5. (Fix + cost bubbles) "One fix: move the due date to the 20th, two days after payday. No fee, no credit hit."
6. (YES, then the draft card) "He replies YES. FirstBill writes the request. He sends it. FirstBill never logs in or touches his money."
7. Close: "ChatGPT answers questions. FirstBill catches the ones you didn't know to ask."

Optional second clip (15 seconds): click **Example 2: forward a notice**. A grad who's already behind forwards a
delinquency letter. Claude decodes it, the credit clock shows 26 days until it's reported, the bank check warns
that paying it all would overdraft, and YES drafts the forbearance request.

Backup plan: if the live link or Wi-Fi fails, the same app runs locally with `npm install && npm run dev`, and the
built-in answers need no internet for Claude.
