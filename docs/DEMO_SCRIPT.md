# PayPing: live demo walkthrough (about 2 minutes)

**What PayPing is (say this first):** "PayPing is a text thread, not an app. It watches your student loans and checking, texts you *before* a bill goes wrong, suggests one fix with its cost, and you approve it with YES. It never moves money. Today it's sandbox data."

**Before you start:** open PayPing from the Home Screen, tap ↻ (top right) to start over. The start screen says "Updated <time>" so you know it's the newest build.

---

## 1. Join by text (20 s)

| You do | You say |
|---|---|
| Type **JOIN** | "No app store, no signup form. You just text JOIN." |
| Type a first name (e.g. **Tariq**) | "It asks your name…" |
| Tap **The 18th** | "…and when you get paid. Payday is the key to everything." |

## 2. Loans and bank, still inside the texts (20 s)

| You do | You say |
|---|---|
| Tap **Attach my aid file** | "You download your aid file from StudentAid.gov and just send it." |
| Tap the blue **MyStudentData.txt** bubble, then **Done** | "This is what was sent: two federal loans, $28,600, with Meridian." |
| Tap **Connect my bank** → **Continue** | "Bank link is Plaid, read-only. PayPing can see balances and bills. It can't move a dollar." |

*Alternative:* tap **Skip, I'll text my numbers**. "If you don't want to link a bank, you don't have to."

## 3. It texts you first (30 s)

It jumps to **"Sunday 9:41 AM · 2 days later"**.

- "Two days later, PayPing texts *first*. Your grace period ends Dec 2."
- "Your first loan payment is **$310.39** and Meridian auto-pulls it **Dec 4**."
- Point at the plain-text breakdown: "Then the phone bill and car insurance hit. You'd be **$23.21 short on Dec 11**. That's an overdraft fee, often $25 to $35."
- "It suggests one idea and asks: move the due date to the **20th**, two days after payday. Same payment, $0, no credit hit."

## 4. You approve, you send (15 s)

| You do | You say |
|---|---|
| Tap **YES** | "It writes the request. Tap to copy, you send it. It never logs in." |

## 5. Payday (15 s)

Automatically: **"Dec 18 · Payday"**

- "On payday it sees the paycheck land and asks: **$25 a month** extra saves about **$891** and you're done **11 months** sooner. **$50** saves about **$1,614**."
- "That math is code, not AI. The AI only explains things in plain English."
- Tap **$25 a month**.

## 6. Pay This First, no bank needed (20 s)

Type: **I have $180. Rent 550, card 40, loan 310, electric 60**

- "Short on cash? Text your bills. It suggests what to pay first: lights and food, then the card, because cards are reported at **30 days** late. The federal loan can wait since it isn't reported until **90**."
- Tap **YES**: "It writes the note to your landlord asking for a payment plan."

## 7. Feels like iMessage (10 s)

- Press and hold any text: tapbacks and Reply/Copy, just like iMessage.
- Tap **‹** (top left): "Every other money app texts everyone the same thing. PayPing texts *you* about *your* bill."

## If a judge types something random

- **HELP**: explains what PayPing does and the keywords.
- **STOP**: unsubscribes. **JOIN** brings you back.
- Gibberish: "Sorry, I didn't catch that," then it repeats the current step.
- Questions like "what's a forbearance?", "is this safe?", "do you sell my data?": answered, then it steers back.

## Closing line

"ChatGPT answers questions. PayPing catches the ones you didn't know to ask."
